
# Volphax Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open, inclusive, respectful, and welcoming environment, we as contributors, maintainers, and members of the Volphax community pledge to make participation in our projects and communities a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

At Volphax, we believe that technology should empower people, businesses, and communities. We are committed to building an environment that encourages collaboration, innovation, mutual respect, and professional integrity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming, respectful, and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Providing constructive and professional feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Encouraging collaboration, innovation, and continuous learning
- Respecting the privacy, security, and intellectual property of others
- Acting with honesty, transparency, and professional integrity

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or bullying
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or electronic address, without explicit permission
- Unauthorized disclosure of confidential information or proprietary source code
- Deliberate disruption of projects, discussions, or community activities
- Impersonation, deception, or malicious behavior
- Retaliation against individuals who report misconduct in good faith
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers and organization administrators are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior and are expected to take appropriate, consistent, and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, documentation, wiki edits, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation by individuals whose behavior is considered inappropriate, threatening, offensive, disruptive, or harmful.

Volphax leadership is responsible for ensuring that this Code of Conduct is applied fairly, consistently, and in good faith across its projects and communities.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all Volphax project spaces, including public and private repositories, organizational teams, communication channels, documentation platforms, and community spaces.

It also applies when an individual is representing Volphax, its projects, or its communities in public spaces.

Examples of representation include:

- Using an official Volphax email address
- Posting through an official social media account
- Participating in events as an appointed representative
- Representing a Volphax product, project, or technology
- Communicating on behalf of the organization

Representation of Volphax and its projects may be further defined and clarified by organization administrators and project maintainers.

This Code of Conduct also applies outside official project spaces when there is a reasonable belief that an individual's behavior may negatively impact Volphax, its projects, or its communities.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, discriminatory, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the Volphax organization administrators or designated project maintainers.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated in good faith and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.

Volphax is committed to protecting the confidentiality of individuals involved in the reporting process, to the extent reasonably possible and consistent with applicable legal obligations.

Retaliation against individuals who report misconduct or participate in an investigation in good faith will not be tolerated.

Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, enforcement actions may include:

- A private warning
- Removal of inappropriate content or contributions
- Temporary restrictions on participation
- Revocation of repository or organization access
- Temporary or permanent removal from projects and communities

Project maintainers or organization administrators who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by Volphax leadership.

Further details of specific enforcement policies may be published separately.

## Governance

Volphax reserves the right to update this Code of Conduct as its projects, technologies, and communities evolve.

Individual projects may establish additional contribution guidelines or community standards, provided that they remain consistent with the principles established in this Code of Conduct.

Participation in Volphax projects and communities requires compliance with the applicable Code of Conduct and project-specific policies.
